1. Introduction

Shoperb is a mobile shopping companion for organising shopping lists, saving loyalty-card details, recording shopping history and prices, storing optional receipt photos, and viewing personal shopping insights. Shoperb is designed to work primarily offline, storing core shopping information locally on your device.

2. Information you provide

Depending on the features you choose, Shoperb may store:

  • Shopping list names, product names, quantities, optional prices and purchased statuses.
  • Completed shopping trips, dates, price corrections and personal shopping insights.
  • Loyalty programme names, card nicknames, card numbers, barcode data and barcode settings.
  • Receipt photographs captured with your camera or selected from your device.
  • Currency, appearance and reminder preferences.

Prices, loyalty-card details and receipt photographs are optional. Shoperb is not designed to store payment-card security codes or banking passwords.

3. How we use information

Shoperb uses this information to maintain your shopping lists, display loyalty-card barcodes, save completed trips, calculate recorded subtotals, suggest previously entered products, display receipts, show personal price trends, and schedule reminders you request. Personal price insights reflect information you enter and are not live supermarket price quotes.

4. Local storage

Shoperb stores core shopping data in a local device database and receipt images, when attached, in app-specific storage. Core features do not require a Shoperb user account or a Shoperb-operated cloud service. Information may also exist in backups or exports you create or in device-level backup services, depending on your device configuration.

5. Camera and photo access

With your permission, Shoperb uses the camera to scan physical loyalty-card barcodes and photograph shopping receipts. You can also select receipt photos from your photo library. These features are initiated by you, and permissions can be managed in your device settings. The current version does not automatically read receipt text or extract prices from photos.

6. Notifications

Shoperb may schedule optional local shopping reminders on your device when requested. You can manage notifications through Android settings. Remote push notifications are not required for core reminders.

7. Sharing and third-party services

Shoperb does not currently offer live, server-synchronised shared lists. If you export or share information using another application or service, that service may handle the information under its own terms. Shoperb also relies on mobile platform features and software libraries, which may have their own technical-data practices. The data practices of third-party software included in the release should be reflected in this policy.

8. Advertising and analytics

The initial version is intended to contain no third-party advertisements. Personal Shopping Insights are calculated from local shopping records; they are not intended as a third-party advertising or behavioural analytics service. If advertising or external analytics are added, this policy will be updated to describe them.

9. Retention and deletion

Locally stored data generally remains until you remove it through available app controls, clear the application's data or uninstall it. Removing a receipt does not necessarily remove the associated trip. Clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently erase stored lists, history and receipts. Exported backups may remain until you delete those copies separately. Shoperb does not currently provide online user accounts.

10. Security

Shoperb uses app-specific storage and Android's application isolation to help protect information stored on your device. No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect access to your device and any backups you export.

11. Children's privacy

Shoperb is a general shopping utility and is not specifically designed for children. It does not offer child accounts or a Shoperb-operated service intended to collect children's personal information.

12. Policy changes

We may update this policy as the app's features or data handling change. The latest policy will be published at this page with an updated effective date.
