1. Introduction

Shoperb is a mobile shopping companion for organising shopping lists, saving loyalty-card details, recording shopping history and prices, storing optional receipt photos, and viewing personal shopping insights. Shoperb is designed to work primarily offline, storing core shopping information locally on your device.

2. Information you provide

Depending on the features you choose, Shoperb may store:

  • Shopping list names, product names (including items you enter or confirm after scanning a shopping list), quantities, optional prices and purchased statuses.
  • Completed shopping trips, dates, price corrections and personal shopping insights.
  • Loyalty programme names, card nicknames, card numbers, barcode data and barcode settings.
  • Receipt photographs captured with your camera or selected from your device.
  • Currency, appearance and reminder preferences.

Prices, loyalty-card details and receipt photographs are optional. Shoperb is not designed to store payment-card security codes or banking passwords.

3. How we use information

Shoperb uses this information to maintain your shopping lists, display loyalty-card barcodes, save completed trips, calculate recorded subtotals, suggest previously entered products, display receipts, show personal price trends, and schedule reminders you request. Personal price insights reflect information you enter and are not live supermarket price quotes.

4. Local storage

Shoperb stores core shopping data in a local device database and receipt images, when attached, in app-specific storage. Core features do not require a Shoperb user account or a Shoperb-operated cloud service. Information may also exist in backups or exports you create or in device-level backup services, depending on your device configuration.

5. Camera, photos and shopping-list scanning

With your permission, Shoperb uses the camera to scan physical loyalty-card barcodes, photograph shopping receipts, and scan a shopping list. You can also choose receipt or shopping-list photos through your device's image picker. These actions are initiated by you. Camera permissions can be managed in Android settings.

Shopping-list text recognition uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition on Android. According to Google's ML Kit documentation, input images and recognised text are processed on your device and are not sent to Google servers by ML Kit. Shoperb does not implement an upload service for these images or recognition results. You review and correct recognised items before saving them to the local shopping database. Images selected or captured for scanning may remain in temporary app/device storage until cleared by the app or operating system. Optional receipt attachments are retained separately in app-specific storage. Shoperb does not automatically extract receipt prices.

On-device recognition is distinct from SDK diagnostics. Google's ML Kit documentation describes collection of technical and usage information, including device model and operating system, app package and version, per-installation identifiers, performance measurements, SDK configuration, input/output sizes, feature events and error information. Google uses these metrics for diagnostics, usage analytics and maintaining and improving its APIs. ML Kit may contact Google services for these purposes and for updates or compatibility information. Google states that this diagnostic information is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. This does not mean that shopping-list images or recognised text are uploaded.

For details, see Google ML Kit terms, ML Kit Android data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

6. Notifications

Shoperb may schedule optional local shopping reminders on your device when requested. You can manage notifications through Android settings. Remote push notifications are not required for core reminders.

7. Sharing, exports and third-party services

Shoperb has no user account, Shoperb-operated cloud database or live server-synchronised shopping lists. Core shopping lists, purchase history, personal prices, loyalty-card details and receipt attachments are stored locally on your device.

You can choose to export a backup or share a shopping list through Android's share menu. A .shoperb shopping-list file contains the list name, product names, quantities, optional unit prices and currency. It does not include loyalty-card data, receipt photos, purchase history or purchased tick states. A backup can include the local data described in this policy, including receipt images where attached. Choose recipients and storage destinations carefully. Receiving applications and storage services handle information under their own privacy policies, and copies outside Shoperb are not removed when you clear Shoperb's local data.

Shoperb also offers an export of its public Open Food Facts derived product catalogue. That catalogue export contains public product information and licensing notices, without personal shopping data. Importing a shopping-list file occurs locally after validation and review.

Opening the Privacy Policy, source or licence links uses your chosen browser and an internet connection. Those websites may process ordinary connection information under their own policies. Google ML Kit's technical-data practices are described in section 5. Other Android and device services remain subject to their respective policies.

8. Advertising, analytics and local insights

Shoperb 1.0.1 does not integrate an advertising SDK or a separate third-party product analytics or crash-reporting service in its application code. Personal Shopping Insights are computed from your local shopping records and are not an advertising analytics service. Google ML Kit may collect technical diagnostic and usage metrics as described in section 5; local shopping functionality should not be interpreted as a promise that no SDK communicates with external services. If additional advertising or analytics services are introduced, this policy will be updated.

9. Retention and deletion

Locally stored data generally remains until you remove it through available app controls, clear the application's data or uninstall it. Removing a receipt does not necessarily remove the associated trip. Clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently erase stored lists, history and receipts. Exported backups may remain until you delete those copies separately. Shoperb does not currently provide online user accounts.

10. Security

Shoperb uses app-specific storage and Android's application isolation to help protect information stored on your device. No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect access to your device and any backups you export.

11. Children's privacy

Shoperb is a general shopping utility and is not specifically designed for children. It does not offer child accounts or a Shoperb-operated service intended to collect children's personal information.

12. Policy changes

We may update this policy as the app's features or data handling change. The latest policy will be published at this page with an updated effective date.
